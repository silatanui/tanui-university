# Tanui University image brief

Generate these images, save them into `university/assets/images/` with the exact filenames below, then refresh the site.

Brand colors to echo subtly (do not overdo orange): charcoal `#2F3437`, accent orange `#E85D04`, stone `#E8E4DE`.

No text overlays, logos, watermarks, or UI chrome in any image.

---

## 1. `hero-campus.jpg`
**Size:** 2400 × 1600 px (landscape, 3:2)

**Show:** Wide dawn or late-afternoon view of a university main quad. Classical or modern academic buildings frame a tree-lined walkway. Students walking naturally in the middle distance (not close portraits). Soft natural light, realistic photography, East African / Kenyan campus atmosphere without looking like a travel postcard.

**Avoid:** Crowds filling the frame, neon colors, heavy fog, gothic European castles.

---

## 2. `academics-lab.jpg`
**Size:** 1600 × 1200 px (4:3)

**Show:** Interior of a computing or engineering teaching lab. Laptops or workstations on long tables, one instructor assisting two students. Bright, clean, contemporary. Shallow depth of field on the near desk.

**Avoid:** Sci-fi neon labs, stock “thumbs up at camera” poses.

---

## 3. `campus-library.jpg`
**Size:** 1600 × 1200 px (4:3)

**Show:** University library reading room with tall windows, wood or concrete shelves, students studying quietly. Warm daylight. Architectural and calm.

**Avoid:** Empty perfect showroom with no people, dark moody cinema lighting.

---

## 4. `student-life.jpg`
**Size:** 1600 × 1200 px (4:3)

**Show:** Outdoor student life on campus: a small group of students talking on steps or under trees between classes. Casual, natural, diverse ages. Optimistic but not staged.

**Avoid:** Party scenes, sports stadium crowds, posed lineup against a wall.

---

## 5. `research-focus.jpg`
**Size:** 1600 × 1200 px (4:3)

**Show:** Close environmental shot of applied research: circuit board, data notebook, or field notes beside a laptop in a quiet lab or studio. Hands optional. Suggest seriousness and craft.

**Avoid:** Generic DNA helix CGI, glowing HUD interfaces.

---

## 6. `admissions-path.jpg`
**Size:** 2000 × 1200 px (5:3)

**Show:** A welcoming entrance path or campus gate with the main academic building visible beyond. Morning light. Feels like arrival on the first day.

**Avoid:** Security barriers, parking lots as the main subject, text on gates.

---
